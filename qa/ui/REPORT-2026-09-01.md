### Checked and clean

- **Packaged path.** `--port 9000` … `9006` all serve, inject the socket URL and connect; a second
  copy on a busy port prints the refusal and exits 1; foreign origin rejected; traversal refused
  (smoke). Not exercised: a stale asset after an upgrade — `no-store` on the shell and `immutable`
  on fingerprinted assets are asserted in `tests/hub.test.ts`, not end to end.
- **Window sizes.** 1100×620, 1280×720, 1366×768, 1920×1080: `documentElement.scrollWidth` equals
  the viewport and a `scrollTo(10000, 10000)` reads back `0,0` at every one; zero console errors
  and warnings in every run (the only console line in the whole session is Chromium's own
  `ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED` when the hub is deliberately killed).
- **States.** No sessions · one session with two messages · finished session · hub down · 121
  agents · transcript rewritten under the hub (`reset` → LOADING → rebuilt, tokens 960 → 0 → 540,
  no double count) · backlog truncated.
- **Volume.** Chat at the 1 000-message cap: 250 cards ≈ 3.5 k DOM nodes, scroll frames p95 23 ms,
  idle p95 21 ms. Widening by 250 costs ~100 ms. **Virtualization is not warranted**; the one
  second-long hitch was the animation flood in #1. The 121-agent room idles at p95 ~40 ms — that is
  the room loop placing 121 marks a frame, not the dock.
- **Keyboard.** One tab stop for the 200-column strip, palette and help trap focus, Escape chain
  intact; every stop paints a ring.

## What changed (§2, §3)

**First run is now designed.** `claude-roundtable --demo` stages the same synthetic session
`npm run demo` did from a clone (`scripts/promo/demoRoom.ts`, extracted from `scripts/demo.ts`,
bundled into `dist/server/cli.mjs`). The root is fixed under the OS temp directory, `--root` is
overridden so the stage can never wipe a real directory, the hub watches it with polling, and the
directory is deleted on exit. In-process test in `tests/cli.test.ts`; smoke step 10. The empty
state (`after/firstrun-*.png`) names the directory being watched, says the first session to start
appears on its own, and gives the command.

**The shell** (`src/index.css`, 463 lines touched; `App.tsx` adds two stage classes):

- Every colour is an OKLCH token; every neutral is tinted toward the room (hue 60–88 by day,
  60 by night); no pure white or black. Contrast measured with `scratchpad/rtui-contrast.mjs`:
  `--ink-3` ≥ 4.5:1 on `--panel` and `--panel-2` in both themes, status colours ≥ 4.5:1 on their
  washes, `--accent-2` demoted to rings and bars.
- Panels are flat sheets with hairlines: the 12 px radius, 30 px shadow and backdrop blur on every
  panel are gone; only what floats (picker, palette, help, inspector) casts.
- The roster is a column down the stage's left edge instead of a card over a corner of the room.
  This also removes the dead band of letterbox the inset used to leave beside a short rail
  (`before/idle-1280-day.png` vs `after/idle-1280-day.png`).
- The stage's letterbox takes a theme token (`--stage-bg`) instead of the renderer's fixed
  near-black.
- The feed is a list under hairlines; the human's turn, a verdict and the selected agent are
  washes, not bordered boxes; the 3 px left stripes are gone.
- Type one step larger throughout (labels 9.5–10 px, body 12.5 px, numbers 13 px), tracked
  uppercase section labels, `--top-h` 44, `--time-h` 66.

**Directions.** Three were rendered on the real app before choosing (`qa/ui/direction-*.png`):
A "oak and paper" (light day, warm-charcoal night, flat, rail column), B "control room" (dark
frame in both themes, hairline grid, no cards), C "gallery" (neutral, airy, floating rail). **A**,
taking B's hairline feed and typographic discipline. B was rejected because it makes the day
theme's shell dark, against the approved daylight mockup; C keeps the dead band and drifts toward
a generic neutral SaaS look.

**Docs.** `README.md` (install flags, the first-run section around `--demo`, the layout sketch,
the OFFLINE paragraph, `npm run smoke`), `SECURITY.md` (the stale "no tags / 0.1.0" and "port is
hardcoded" claims corrected; the Origin gate paragraph names the hub's own origins; a paragraph on
`--demo` as the one mode that writes, and where), `CHANGELOG.md` (an Unreleased section).

## Left undone, and why

- **e2e ran on 4173, not 5173.** Port 5173 on this machine belonged to another project's Vite dev
  server; with `reuseExistingServer` the suite would have adopted it. A
  scratch config (`scratchpad/rtui-playwright.config.ts`) runs the same spec against Vite on
  4173, which the hub's gate already names: 6/6, polling watcher. The native-watcher leg is CI's.
- **The room is untouched.** Two things seen and left: thought bubbles from three neighbouring
  desks overlap each other (`after/busy-1920-night.png`), and the blit is bottom-aligned, so a
  band sits above the room on every non-16:9 stage. Both are `src/office/pixel/*` under the
  contract and neither is a defect the brief asked for.
- **Chat virtualization**: not done, on the numbers above.
- **`TOOLS 400`** in the dock tab is the tools window's cap, printed like a total. Documented in
  `App.tsx` as intended; left.
- **The 121-agent frame time** (~40 ms p95) is the room loop, not the shell; left.

## The one thing to do next

Push, watch CI's eight legs, and cut `0.2.1` with `--demo` in it: the empty office was the first
thing every stranger saw, and it is now the last thing they need to see. After that, the room's
